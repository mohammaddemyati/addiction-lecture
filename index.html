1. What IS addiction?

Addiction is a chronic brain disorder—not a moral failing—characterised by compulsive seeking and use despite harm. It involves changes in reward, motivation and memory circuits.

“It’s the only disease that tries to convince you that you don’t have it.” — Anonymous

2. The addicted brain (90-second explainer)

  • Dopamine surge → “salience tagging”
  • Pre-frontal cortex ↓ impulse control weakens
  • Amygdala & stress circuits hijacked

4. The addiction cycle

  1. Binge / Intoxication
  2. Withdrawal / Negative affect
  3. Preoccupation / Anticipation

…and round it goes, deepening the neuro-adaptations each lap.

5. Recovery & evidence-based treatment

Medication

Opioid-agonist therapy, Naltrexone, etc.

CBT / MI

Change talk, coping skills, relapse prevention.

Community

12-step, SMART, harm-reduction groups.

Recovery is remission of symptoms + improved quality of life.
